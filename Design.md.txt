# ClauseLens AI — Design System

## 1. Design Goal

ClauseLens AI should feel:

- Simple
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Calm
- Professional
- Human
- Easy for non-lawyers

It must NOT look like:

- A complicated law firm system
- A government portal
- An old enterprise application
- A generic AI chatbot

---

# 2. Design Inspiration

The experience should combine:

- Modern SaaS simplicity
- Document applications
- AI assistants
- Premium productivity tools

The user should immediately understand:

"Upload a document and ask what it means."

---

# 3. Brand Personality

Keywords:

CLEAR
TRUSTWORTHY
INTELLIGENT
SIMPLE
CALM
HELPFUL

Avoid aggressive AI aesthetics.

Avoid excessive gradients.

Avoid excessive glassmorphism.

Avoid neon colors.

Avoid unnecessary animations.
