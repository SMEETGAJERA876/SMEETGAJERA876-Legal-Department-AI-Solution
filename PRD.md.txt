# ClauseLens AI — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

ClauseLens AI is an AI-powered legal document understanding and navigation platform.

The product helps ordinary people understand long and complicated legal documents without requiring them to know legal terminology.

Users can upload a legal document and ask questions such as:

- "What is my notice period?"
- "When does this agreement expire?"
- "How much do I have to pay?"
- "What happens if I leave early?"
- "Who is responsible for maintenance?"
- "Can the agreement be renewed?"
- "Where does it mention termination?"
- "Show me all payment-related clauses."

The system finds the relevant information, explains it in simple language, and takes the user directly to the supporting page and clause in the original document.

The system is an INFORMATION AND DOCUMENT-NAVIGATION TOOL.

It is NOT a lawyer and must NOT present itself as a replacement for legal professionals.

---

# 2. Problem

Legal documents are often:

- Long
- Written in difficult language
- Filled with legal terminology
- Poorly structured from a normal user's perspective
- Difficult to search
- Difficult to compare
- Difficult to understand without professional assistance

A normal PDF search only searches words.

For example, if the user searches:

"notice period"

but the document says:

"Either party may terminate this agreement by providing ninety (90) days written notice."

A traditional search may not understand that this is the notice-period information.

ClauseLens AI should understand the meaning behind the user's question.

---

# 3. Product Vision

Make legal documents easier to understand and navigate.

The core experience is:

UPLOAD → SEARCH → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → ACT

The user should always be able to see where the information came from.

Future:

# 6.2 Document Processing

The system extracts:

- Page number
- Text
- Paragraphs
- Sections
- Clauses

Page information MUST be preserved.

The system must never lose the relationship:

Document → Page → Text → Clause

---

# 6.3 Document Overview

After processing, display:

- Document name
- Number of pages
- Document type
- Parties if detectable
- Important dates
- Important amounts
- Notice period
- Termination information
- Payment information
- Important obligations

---

# 6.4 Exact Search

Users can search exact words.

Example:

"notice"

Return:

- Page number
- Matching text
- Context

---

# 6.5 Semantic Search

Users can ask questions naturally.

Example:

"What happens if I want to leave the agreement?"

The system should retrieve relevant information related to:

- Termination
- Notice
- Early termination
- Expiry
- Renewal
- Penalty

even when the exact search phrase is not present.

# 6.7 Ask Your Document

Users can ask:

"What is the notice period?"

"What happens if I terminate early?"

"When does the contract expire?"

"Who pays maintenance?"

"How much is the security deposit?"

The AI must answer using retrieved document evidence.

---

# 6.8 Evidence and Citations

Every factual AI response should show:

- Page
- Clause
- Source text

Example:

Answer:
90 days.

Source:
Page 7 · Clause 12

Original text:
"Either party may terminate..."

The user should be able to click:

"View source"

and jump directly to the document.

---

# 6.9 PDF Navigation

The PDF viewer must support:

- Page navigation
- Zoom
- Search
- Jump to page
- Source navigation
- Highlighting where technically possible

---

# 6.10 Clause Highlighting

When the system identifies a relevant clause:

1. Open the relevant page.
2. Locate the source text.
3. Highlight it.
4. Display the explanation beside it.

Never pretend to highlight text if the system cannot actually locate it.

---

# 6.11 Simple Explanation

Every important result can be displayed as:

### What the document says

Original legal wording.

### In simple language

Plain-language explanation.

### Why it matters

Short contextual explanation.

### Source

Page and clause.

---

# 6.12 Related Information

When a user searches:

"Early termination"

show related concepts:

- Notice period
- Termination
- Penalty
- Renewal
- Contract duration
- Deposit

---

# 6.13 Questions for a Legal Professional

Generate questions based on the document.

Example:

"What happens if the 90-day notice cannot be completed?"

The system should NOT answer with legal advice.

Instead:

"Questions you may want to discuss with a qualified legal professional."

---

# 6.14 Contract Comparison

Users can upload two versions.

Example:

Contract A
Contract B

System identifies:

- Added clauses
- Removed clauses
- Modified clauses
- Changed amounts
- Changed dates
- Changed notice periods
- Changed obligations

---

# 7. User Experience Principles

The product must be understandable to a person who has never studied law.

Avoid unnecessary legal terminology.

Use:

"Notice period"

instead of:

"Contractual termination notice obligation"

Use:

"Money you may need to pay"

instead of:

"Financial liability"

when appropriate.

# 9. Safety Requirements

ClauseLens AI must:

- Provide information rather than professional legal advice.
- Clearly identify document-derived information.
- Provide source references.
- Say when information cannot be found.
- Never fabricate clauses.
- Never fabricate dates.
- Never fabricate financial values.
- Never invent legal rules.
- Never guarantee legal outcomes.
- Never claim to be a lawyer.

---

# 10. Success Criteria

The MVP is successful when a user can:

1. Upload a PDF.
2. See processing progress.
3. View the document.
4. Search for a concept.
5. Get a relevant answer.
6. See the exact source page.
7. Navigate to the source.
8. Understand the clause in simple language.
9. Ask follow-up questions.
10. Generate questions for a legal professional.

- Court outcome prediction
