# 1. PROJECT IDENTITY

## Project Name

**ClauseLens AI**

## Product Type

Universal Legal & Official Document Intelligence Platform.

## Core Purpose

ClauseLens AI helps ordinary people understand, search, navigate, compare, and extract information from:

* Government documents
* Legal documents
* Contracts
* Employment documents
* Court documents
* Company documents
* Public notices
* Financial documents
* Property documents
* Education documents
* Policy and regulatory documents
* Other official documents

## Core Principle

```text
Document
    ↓
Understand
    ↓
Search
    ↓
Extract
    ↓
Verify
    ↓
Explain
    ↓
Source
    ↓
Navigate
```

The most important product principle is:

> **Evidence → Source → Explanation → User Understanding**

ClauseLens AI is an information and document-understanding system, not a replacement for a qualified legal professional.

# 12. AI / RAG RULE

AI answers must be grounded in retrieved document evidence.

The AI must NOT answer based on unsupported assumptions.

If the document does not contain the requested information:

```text
I couldn't find this information in the uploaded document.
```

Never invent:

* clauses
* dates
* amounts
* obligations
* rights
* penalties
* deadlines
* legal conclusions

# 14. LEGAL SAFETY

ClauseLens AI provides:

```text
Legal information
Document understanding
Document navigation
Document comparison
Information extraction
Question preparation
```

It does NOT provide:

```text
Professional legal representation
Guaranteed legal outcomes
Court outcome prediction
Legal strategy guarantees
Definitive professional legal advice
```

Avoid statements such as:

```text
"This is definitely illegal."
"You will win this case."
"You should definitely sue."
```

Use evidence-based wording such as:

```text
"According to the uploaded document..."
"The document states..."
"I couldn't find..."
```
